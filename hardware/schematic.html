Greenhouse Controller — Schematic Prototype

A single-page circuit reference for the ESP32-C3 Super Mini greenhouse automation board, consolidating HARDWARE_CONNECTIONS.md and config_hardware.h into one drawing. This is the basis for the KiCad schematic/PCB files that follow — not a physical footprint layout.

01 Power distribution

One 12V supply is stepped down twice. The split matters for the board layout: 12V feeds the switched loads directly, 5V feeds motor/coil-side and card logic, and 3.3V is the sensor and relay-logic rail — mixing these up is what damages the ESP32-C3's I/O.

02 ESP32-C3 pin assignment

All 16 assigned GPIOs, color-coded by net class. GPIO 8 and 9 carry boot-strapping constraints (LED loading, BOOT button) documented in config_hardware.h — both are safe here because relays default active-LOW/HIGH-off, matching the pins' natural boot state.

03 Protection circuits

Six passive networks stand between the ESP32-C3 and the outside world. Skipping any one of these is what produces the failure modes already logged in the wiring doc: -127°C readings, CRC errors, boot-loop into download mode, or a fried GPIO.

04 Bill of materials

Grouped by role. Passive values match the protection insets above; buy resistors in 1% tolerance for the flow-meter dividers since the 3.33V target sits close to the 3.6V absolute max.
